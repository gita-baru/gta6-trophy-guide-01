Save strategy — no mission replay, no New Game+

There are 15 manual save slots + 1 autosave. Save at the start and end of every chapter (10 saves), keep 5 rotating saves for free-roam cleanup, and keep one save before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies AND saving — do not use them on this playthrough. (Source: PowerPyx)

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

Chapter 1, Jason's home balcony. After the mission “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she says she will meditate, then goes to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. It raises your meditation counter +1, but it is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the others, and it is not yet confirmed whether this meditation is required for the trophy — do it anyway, and keep a Chapter 1 save. (Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase)

All 18 meditation spots across 10 locations count for the trophy; meditating together as Jason + Lucia at one spot counts +2. 100% completion only needs 15 of 18.

No other missable trophy

Missed optional missions, a temporarily unavailable quest giver (e.g. Kenton), a permanently lost fence and story-building magazine collections do not affect any trophy or 100%. Every trophy-related quest giver is available again after the story. (Source: PowerPyx)

One caution, not a trophy: Yago's “Race Against Yago” in Chapter 3 is time-sensitive (hourglass icon) and counts toward the Strangers & Freaks strand — do it as soon as it appears. Sources disagree on whether it matters for trophies, so treat it as a warning, not a certainty. (Sources: GTABase/OutputLag vs PowerPyx)

Manual-save plan

15 manual slots + 1 autosave. Save at the start and end of each of the 5 chapters (10 saves), rotate the other 5 for free-roam cleanup, and save once more before the final mission. No mission replay exists, so a Chapter 1 save is your only insurance for the balcony meditation. (Sources: PowerPyx, OutputLag)
