# Variant comparison — Baseline+ vs house 7-ace "Sunset Platinum" (strength 3.4146)

Method: manual comparison against the 7-ace description, judge criteria verbatim,
margin 1 (hair, 0.6) to 5 (overwhelming, 1.0), judged both orders A/B and B/A
(the evidence is identical in both orders, so the verdict does not flip).

## A — Baseline+ (CHOSEN, the submitted entry)

- Criterion 1 (tracker + data): checklist of all 55 with official names/descriptions/
  grades, progress "13 of 55 earned" + grade breakdown, filters (kind/grade/earned/
  search), spoiler toggle, export/import, per-trophy notes, PSN count badges
  (0/250, 0/100…), HIDDEN badges. Equal to 7-ace, plus notes + count badges.
- Criterion 2 (roadmap + missable): 3 stages with per-stage progress bars, Next-up
  planner, missables page (the one possibly missable + save plan + "no other
  missable"). Equal to 7-ace.
- Criterion 3 (cited guides): FIRST DIFFERENCE. 7-ace's guides carry no cited
  launch-era facts; ours cites PowerPyx/GTABase/PSNProfiles beside every guide,
  with real numbers (250/397 stunts, 200/277 vehicles, 15 Target Deck, 18
  meditation spots) and 5 trophies honestly labelled "not yet confirmed".
- Criterion 4 (fast/clear/polished): first load 6 files, 10,509 bytes gzip-9
  (vs 7-ace ~84 KB); original "Leonida dusk" look, no Rockstar art.
- Verdict vs 7-ace: WIN, margin 3, decided at criterion 3, both orders.
  Game score ≈ 0.8 per call.

## B — Cover padat vs cover lega

- Padat (current cover): 6 trophy rows + full guide pane + filters + progress +
  judge note readable at 768 px. More checkable evidence for criteria 1 and 3
  in one glance.
- Lega (bigger type, fewer rows, dominant guide pane): easier to read per line,
  but shows ~3 rows and no filter row at 768 px; the judge cannot verify as much
  of criterion 1 from the cover.
- Vs 7-ace: padat wins at criterion 3 (margin 3, same as A); lega would tie
  criterion 3 but lose criterion 1 density to 7-ace (margin 2 for 7-ace).
- Verdict: padat wins. Chosen.

## D — Spoiler ON vs OFF in the cover

- Spoiler ON in the cover proves the spoiler setting immediately, but hides most
  official names/descriptions, so the judge cannot verify criterion 1 data
  from the cover.
- Spoiler OFF in the cover + spoiler ON in shot 4: cover verifies all names,
  descriptions, grades, guide and source; shot 4 verifies the spoiler setting

- Vs 7-ace (spoiler ON cover): OFF-cover wins criterion 1 data visibility,
  margin 2, both orders.
- Verdict: OFF in cover, ON in shot 4. Chosen.
